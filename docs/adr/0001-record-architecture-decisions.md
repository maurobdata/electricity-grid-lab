# 1. Record architecture decisions

Date: 2026-08-22 · Status: Accepted

## Context

This project is deliberately experimental. The product it will eventually serve is not
decided, and several plausible products contradict each other. Decisions made now will be
revisited by a future reader — possibly under time pressure on 11 September — who needs to
know not just *what* was chosen but *what it was chosen instead of*, and *what would make
it wrong*.

## Decision

Record every meaningful architectural or product decision as a short ADR in `docs/adr/`,
numbered sequentially. An ADR states context, the decision, and consequences — including
the conditions under which it should be reversed.

## Consequences

Cheap to write, cheap to read, and it makes reversal a deliberate act rather than an
accident. ADRs are never edited after acceptance; they are superseded by later ones.
